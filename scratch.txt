Indication:
Cervical radiculopathy. 
 
Technique:
Multiplanar multisequence noncontrast MR images of the cervical spine.
 
Comparison:
None. 
 
Findings:
Mild reversal the cervical lordosis. Vertebral heights maintained. No acute fracture or spondylolisthesis. No T1 hypointense lesions or marrow edema. The cervical cord is normal in signal intensity.
 
C2-3: No spinal canal or neural foraminal narrowing.
C3-4: Disc degeneration. Posterior disc osteophyte complex indents the cord. Left uncinate spurring. Mild spinal canal narrowing. Moderate left without right neural foraminal narrowing.
C4-5: No spinal canal or neural foraminal narrowing.
C5-6: Shallow posterior disc osteophyte complex. Right greater than left uncinate spurring. No spinal canal narrowing. Moderately severe right and minimal left neural foraminal narrowing.
C6-7: Mild disc height loss. Shallow posterior disc osteophyte complex. Uncinate spurring. Thickening ligamentum flavum. Minimal spinal canal narrowing. Moderate right and moderately severe left neural foraminal stenosis. 
C7-T1: Minimal facet arthropathy. No spinal canal or neural foraminal narrowing 
T1-2: No spinal canal or neural foraminal narrowing. 
 
Impression:
1. Multilevel cervical spondylosis without high-grade spinal canal stenosis. 
2. At C5-6, moderately severe right neural foraminal stenosis. 
3. At C6-7, moderately severe left and moderate right neural foraminal stenosis.
